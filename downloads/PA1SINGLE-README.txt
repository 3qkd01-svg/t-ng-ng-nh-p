PA1SINGLE 1.0.0 - chi mot luong bam giao dien

1. Dung ban PA1DUAL2 cu truoc khi mo ban nay. Mo start-pa1single.cmd.
   Can Node.js 22+ va Edge/Chrome tren Windows duoc cac phan mem nay ho tro.
   ZIP da co day du ma/launcher/lockfile; npm ci chi tai playwright-core 1.63.0.
2. Nhap link HTTPS cong khai, chon Edge/Chrome va Windows/Mobile tieu chuan.
   Enter o link se dung hay8888.ink; neu link da doi, hay dan link dang dung.
   Mobile la UA/man hinh/cam ung Pixel 7, van la Chromium tren Windows.
   Tu dang nhap, xac minh CAPTCHA/OTP. Cho toi da 120 giay sau buoc mo trang;
   dang nhap som thi bat dau ngay. Het han thi dung, chua bat dau lich.
   Nhan Enter trong cua so chuong trinh de dung. Khong gui mat khau/cookie cho ai.
3. Lich 11/13/15/17/19/21 gio Ha Noi, het ngay thi cho 11h ngay hom sau.
   Lan dau ghe chat ngan roi ve trang chu neu con du thoi gian.
   Moi phien vao chat truoc 30 giay; khong vao truoc 5 phut.
4. KHONG DU DOAN ID, KHONG CO LUONG GUI API, KHONG CAN PHIEN HOC MOC ID.
   Ngay phien dau: cho hong bao moi dau tien tu chat, bam hong bao va Mo.
   Yeu cau HTTP Mo la do trang tu gui sau cu bam; chuong trinh chi quan sat.
   Toi da 2 cu bam Mo cho CUNG hong bao: lan dau va them mot lan sau 500ms
   neu trang cho phep. Khong pha khoa dang mo; dung khi da co phan hoi.
   Khong tu gui lai HTTP khi timeout. Khong mo hong bao thu hai trong phien.
5. Theo doi mat ket noi ngay ca khi dang cho o trang chu.
   Cho 12 giay cho trang tu noi lai. Toi da 3 lan khoi phuc o trang chu va
   3 lan trong khung chat, moi lan cach it nhat 15 giay. Khong gui don.
   O trang chu: reload van giu trang chu; khong tu vao chat som.
   Khong bat dau reload trang chu trong 20 giay truoc luc vao chat.
6. Khong dieu huong khi yeu cau lay token/lich su dang cho, trang dang tai,
   hoac socket moi dang mo. Cho them thoi gian khoi tao sau khi trang tai.
   Token/lich su tre qua 20 giay, document qua 30 giay duoc xet khoi phuc
   lai trong gioi han; khong chu dong xin token.
   XHR lay token do trang tao duoc cho it nhat 20s; XHR Mo it nhat 30s
   de tranh huy som o moc 10s. Khong doi timeout cac endpoint khac.
   Trang van co the tu huy request vi ly do khac; khong tu gui lai.
7. Khong tu dieu huong tu 15 giay truoc den 5 giay sau moc phat.
   Khong reload khi da chon hong bao hoac gui Mo de tranh mat giao dien/phan hoi.
   Chi bao CHAT SAN SANG khi co phong chat, dang nhap va du lieu WebSocket.
   Doi duong dan KHONG duoc tinh la ket noi thanh cong.
8. Giam anh/video/tracker, giu tai nguyen nut Mo va xac minh dang nhap.
   Chi 1 tab, 1 phien dang nhap. Dong ho lich lay tu VPS, hien thi gio Ha Noi.
9. Sau khi phien ket thuc, giu chat 5 phut roi ve trang chu cho phien tiep.
   Cua so cho hong bao moi dai 60 giay; yeu cau Mo da gui duoc cho phan hoi.
10. Bao cao reports/pa1single-....json: ket noi, khoi phuc, ID thuc te,
    thoi diem bam/gui, HTTP/code/loi va do tre. Khong luu mat khau, cookie,
    token, chu ky, noi dung chat thuong hoac body phan hoi.
    Thoi gian co Z la UTC; cong 7 gio de doc theo gio Ha Noi.
11. Ma HTTP/ung dung chi la ket qua quan sat, khong tu suy doan y nghia.
    Timeout khong chung minh may chu chua xu ly. Kiem tra lich su tren trang.
    Chua the bao dam het nghen khi dich vu chat/token phia xa khong phan hoi.
12. Ban nay duoc kiem thu tren may chu mo phong; chua nhan that tu Codex.
    Khong vuot CAPTCHA, khong tu doi mien, khong sao chep xac thuc.
